# Plans and billing

Kestrel offers three subscription plans. You can change plans at any time from **Settings > Subscription**.

## Plan overview

**Starter** costs 12 USD per month and includes unlimited invoices, 1 seat and online payments with a 0.5% platform fee.

**Growth** costs 29 USD per month and adds recurring invoices, automatic reminders, 5 seats, required 2FA and no platform fee.

**Scale** costs 79 USD per month and adds 20 seats, API access, webhooks, custom invoice domains and priority support.

Paying yearly gives you two months free on any plan.

## Free trial

New workspaces get a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan. No card is required to start. When the trial ends, the workspace moves to the Starter plan unless you choose a paid plan, and Growth features such as recurring invoices are paused.

## Upgrading and downgrading

Upgrades take effect immediately, and you are charged a prorated amount for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. If your team has more members than the new plan's seat limit, you need to remove members before the downgrade can be scheduled.

## Payment and receipts

Kestrel charges your subscription to a credit or debit card. Scale customers paying yearly can also pay by bank transfer. Receipts are emailed to the workspace owner and are available in **Settings > Subscription > Billing history**.

## Canceling your subscription

Select **Cancel subscription** in **Settings > Subscription**. Your plan stays active until the end of the paid period, then the workspace becomes read-only. Kestrel does not offer refunds for partial months or unused time on yearly plans.
