# Team members and roles

Invite colleagues, contractors and your accountant to your Kestrel workspace and control what each person can do.

## Inviting a team member

Go to **Settings > Team** and select **Invite member**. Enter their email address and choose a role. The invitation link is valid for 7 days. You can resend or revoke a pending invitation from the same page.

## Roles and permissions

Each member has exactly one role.

- **Owner**: full access, including billing, deleting the workspace and transferring ownership. Each workspace has one owner.
- **Admin**: can manage invoices, clients, payments, settings and team members, but cannot change the subscription or delete the workspace.
- **Accountant**: can view everything, export data and record manual payments, but cannot create or send invoices.
- **Viewer**: read-only access to invoices and clients. Viewers cannot see payout or bank details.

## Seat limits

The Starter plan includes 1 seat, the Growth plan includes 5 seats and the Scale plan includes 20 seats. Accountant seats do not count towards the limit on any plan. Additional seats on Growth and Scale cost 8 USD per month each.

## Transferring ownership

Only the current owner can transfer ownership. Open **Settings > Team**, select an existing Admin and choose **Make owner**. The previous owner becomes an Admin. The new owner must have 2FA enabled before the transfer can complete.

## Removing a member

Select **Remove** next to the member. They lose access immediately, but invoices and notes they created stay in the workspace.
