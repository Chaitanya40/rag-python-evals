# Exporting and deleting your data

You own your data in Kestrel. This article explains how to export it and what happens to it after you cancel or delete your workspace.

## Exporting to CSV

Go to **Reports > Export** and choose invoices, clients, payments or credit notes. Select a date range and download a CSV file. Exports with more than 10,000 rows are prepared in the background and emailed to you as a download link that expires after 72 hours.

## Downloading invoice PDFs

To download PDFs of many invoices at once, filter the invoice list, select the invoices and choose **Download PDFs**. Kestrel creates a ZIP file with up to 500 invoices per download.

## Data after cancellation

When a subscription is canceled, the workspace stays read-only for 12 months. During that time you can sign in, view invoices and export data, but you cannot send invoices or accept payments. After 12 months, Kestrel permanently deletes the workspace data. Invoices are kept for 12 months so you can meet record-keeping obligations, but you are responsible for keeping your own copies for longer retention periods required by law.

## Deleting your workspace

The workspace owner can delete the workspace immediately from **Settings > Workspace > Delete workspace**. You must cancel any active subscription first. Deletion is permanent after a 7-day grace period, during which you can contact support to restore the workspace.

## Deleting a client's personal data

To handle a data deletion request from one of your clients, open the client and choose **More > Anonymize client**. Kestrel removes the client's name, email and address from all invoices but keeps the amounts and dates for your records.
