# Account security

Keep your Kestrel account secure with a strong password, two-factor authentication and regular reviews of active sessions.

## Resetting your password

On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password** and enter your email address. Kestrel sends a reset link that expires after 30 minutes. If you signed up with Google, you do not have a Kestrel password; sign in with Google instead.

If the reset email does not arrive, check your spam folder and confirm that you are using the email address on the account. Workspace owners can also send a password reset link to any member from **Settings > Team**.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a one-time code from an authenticator app to every sign-in. Turn it on in **Profile > Security > Two-factor authentication** and scan the QR code with an app such as 1Password, Authy or Google Authenticator. SMS codes are not supported.

Workspace owners on the Growth and Scale plans can require 2FA for every member.

### Recovery codes

When you enable 2FA, Kestrel shows ten single-use recovery codes. Store them somewhere safe. If you lose your phone, sign in with a recovery code and then set up 2FA again on a new device. If you have lost both your phone and your recovery codes, contact support from your account email address; identity verification takes up to 3 business days.

## Active sessions

**Profile > Security > Sessions** lists every browser and device signed in to your account. Select **Sign out** next to a session you do not recognize, or **Sign out of all other sessions** after you change your password.
