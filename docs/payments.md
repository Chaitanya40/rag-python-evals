# Accepting payments

Kestrel lets clients pay invoices online. Payments are processed by Stripe, which you connect once from your workspace.

## Supported payment methods

Clients can pay with credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard and American Express), Apple Pay and Google Pay. Clients in the United States can also pay by ACH bank transfer, and clients in the eurozone can pay by SEPA Direct Debit. Kestrel does not accept PayPal, checks or cryptocurrency through the online payment page. You can record those payments manually.

## Recording a manual payment

If a client pays by cash, check or a bank transfer outside Kestrel, open the invoice and select **Record payment**. Enter the amount, date and method. Manual payments update the invoice status but do not move any money.

## Processing fees

Kestrel does not add its own fee on top of Stripe's processing fees on the Growth and Scale plans. On the Starter plan, Kestrel charges an additional 0.5% platform fee per online payment. Fees are deducted from each payout, and you can see the breakdown in **Payments > Fees**.

## Payout timing

Stripe pays out your balance to your bank account on a rolling schedule. For most countries, card payments arrive 2 business days after the payment. ACH payments take up to 5 business days, and the first payout for a new Stripe account can take 7 to 14 days while Stripe verifies your business.

## Partial payments and deposits

You can allow clients to pay part of an invoice by turning on **Accept partial payments** before you send it. To request a deposit, set a deposit percentage; the client pays the deposit first and the invoice stays open until the remaining balance is paid.
