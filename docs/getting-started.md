# Getting started with Kestrel

Kestrel is invoicing and payments software for freelancers and small businesses. This guide walks you through setting up your workspace and sending your first invoice.

## Create your workspace

Sign up at the Kestrel website with your email address or a Google account. After you verify your email, Kestrel asks for your business name, country and default currency. Your country controls which tax settings and payment methods are available, and it cannot be changed after the workspace is created. If you entered the wrong country, delete the workspace and create a new one before you send any invoices.

The default currency is used for reports and new invoices. You can still issue individual invoices in other currencies.

## Add your business details

Open **Settings > Business profile** to add your logo, address, tax ID and the payment terms that appear at the bottom of every invoice. The logo must be a PNG or JPG file under 2 MB. Kestrel resizes it to fit the invoice header.

## Send your first invoice

1. Go to **Invoices** and select **New invoice**.
2. Choose an existing client or add a new one with a name and billing email.
3. Add line items with a description, quantity and unit price.
4. Select **Preview** to check the layout, then **Send**.

Your client receives an email with a link to view and pay the invoice online. You can track whether the invoice has been opened from the invoice's activity timeline.

## Next steps

Connect a payment provider so clients can pay by card, and invite your accountant or team members to the workspace.
